What Weak to Week does

Weak to Week turns your paycheck into a simple weekly spending number. It shows how much you have left this week after bills, keeps track of bills as you pay them, and shows your debt payoff dates. The goal is to stop living paycheck to paycheck and start living week to week, with room to spare.

Common questions

Where is my budget saved?

Only on your iPhone or iPad. There's no account and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy for details.

Who is John Smith?

John is a made-up sample budget, so you can look around before entering your own numbers. When you're ready, tap Start my own on Home, or go to More › Settings › Your budget › Start my own budget. Everything is unlocked while John's sample is loaded so you can try every page.

How do I back up or restore my budget?

Go to More › Settings › Your data › Save a full backup. It makes one file with everything in it. Save it to Files or send it to yourself.

To bring a backup back, go to More › Settings › Your data › Restore from a backup and pick the file. It replaces what's in the app now.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my budget?

Set up the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, and your budget comes along with the app. Make sure your old phone has a recent backup first.

You can also save a full backup on the old phone, send it to yourself, and use Restore from a backup on the new one.

What do I get with Plus?

Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks:

  • Every Dollar, What If, Forecast, Tax refund, Charts, Habits and Ask
  • As many bills and debts as you need (free holds 10 bills and 1 debt)
  • Export to a spreadsheet

All looks and themes are free for everyone.

I bought Plus but it's locked again.

Go to More › Settings › Weak to Week Plus and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. This also works on a new phone or on your iPad.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments, so refunds go through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find Weak to Week and choose Request a refund.

How do I clear my spending or start over?

In More › Settings › Your data you can clear all spending and keep your bills, debts and settings. In Your budget you can start a new budget from scratch. Neither one can be undone, so save a backup first.

A number looks wrong.

Check your paycheck amount, next payday and bank balance in More › Settings › Money coming in first. Most surprises come from one of those. If it still looks off, email us with a screenshot of the page.

Not financial advice

Weak to Week helps you plan and track your own money. It isn't financial, tax, legal or investment advice, and its numbers are only as good as what you enter. For big decisions, like a debt plan or your taxes, talk with a qualified professional.
