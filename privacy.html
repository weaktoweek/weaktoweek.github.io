Weak to Week Budgeting

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

The short version

  • Your budget stays on your device.
  • We don't collect, see, sell or share any of your information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

Weak to Week keeps your budget on your iPhone or iPad. That includes your paychecks, bills, debts, spending, categories, people's names and settings. It is saved in the app's own storage on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple may include the app's data in that backup under your own iCloud account. We can't see it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in and no servers of its own. It doesn't use analytics, advertising, crash reporting from outside companies, or tracking of any kind. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera.

Ask and Siri

The Ask page answers questions about your budget using Apple's on-device intelligence when your device supports it. When it doesn't, the app works the answer out itself. Either way, your questions and budget stay on your device. Siri shortcuts for Weak to Week are handled by Apple on your device in the same way.

Buying Plus

Weak to Week Plus is a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, card or Apple Account details. The app only learns from Apple whether Plus is unlocked.

Backups and spreadsheets you share

If you make a backup file or export a spreadsheet, the app creates the file and opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as Files, Mail or Numbers. After that, it is covered by the privacy terms of the place you sent it.

Deleting your information

You can clear your spending or start a fresh budget in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

Weak to Week isn't made for children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting information, this page will say so before that update is released.

Questions about privacy? Email weaktoweek@gmail.com.
